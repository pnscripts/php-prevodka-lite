# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- First release: SEPA credit transfer files in ISO 20022 pain.001.001.09 from PHP code. IBAN checks
  for the 41 SEPA countries, BIC format, Bulgarian transliteration by the Transliteration Act and the
  SEPA character set, exact control sums in cents, amounts read from Bulgarian or English notation,
  category and purpose codes, problems in English and Bulgarian.
